# Orin — Operating Model

*How Orin behaves when money, pressure, opportunity, fear, and client power enter the room.*

Orin is stewardship, not identity.

God is the source. Clients are channels. Work is one means of provision,
not the ground of security. Orin exists to build useful systems for real
businesses, but the business itself does not get to become the thing that
proves Warren's worth.

The commercial model matters. The inner posture matters more.

## 1. Source

Orin receives work as provision and responsibility.

A client win is provision through a channel, not proof that God is near.
A client loss is a closed channel, not proof that provision has failed.
The work is held seriously, but with open hands.

The question before each opportunity is:

> Is this mine to do faithfully, or am I grasping at it because I am afraid?

## 2. Work

Work is good. Toil is the warning sign.

Orin works diligently, builds properly, follows through, tells the truth,
and does the unglamorous parts of the job. Hard work is not the problem.
Anxious self-sufficiency is.

Toil sounds like:

> If I stop, everything will fall apart.

Kingdom work sounds like:

> I will do what is mine to do today, and leave outcomes with God.

## 3. Money

Pricing is an act of clarity.

Orin prices fixed where possible, against outcomes rather than fear,
availability, or a need to be validated. Discounting under pressure is
not generosity. It is usually anxiety wearing a commercial costume.

Generosity is deliberate. It is chosen before pressure enters the room,
bounded clearly, and never used to avoid the discomfort of stating the
real price.

The rule is:

> Price honestly. Give deliberately. Do neither from fear.

## 4. Clients

Orin tells the truth without humiliating people.

The work often exposes drift, inconsistency, waste, duplicated effort,
and decisions nobody remembers making. Those findings can make good
people feel foolish if they are handled badly.

So Orin names the fault line without making anyone the villain.

- Developers are burdened, not stupid.
- Design drift is a system failure, not a character failure.
- The buyer is responsible, but not shamed.
- Orin brings clarity without humiliation.

This is not softness. It is discipline.

## 5. Power

Orin does not hoard power.

The client gets a working system in their own repository. The report
keeps telling the truth after Orin leaves. The router, tokens, guardrails,
and delivery loop exist so the client can keep building without needing
Warren to interpret everything by hand.

The Retainer is care, not captivity.

## 6. Rest

Sabbath is an operating constraint.

Orin does not run on the belief that everything depends on Warren.
There will always be unfinished work. Rest begins because God says it is
time to stop, not because every open loop has been closed.

A week that cannot survive rest is telling the truth about its source.

## 7. Discernment

Before a yes, ask:

- Does this fit the manifesto?
- Can I do this with integrity?
- Does this serve the client well?
- Does this honour my household responsibilities?
- Am I saying yes from responsibility or fear?
- Would I still make this decision if I trusted God for provision?
- What would obedience look like if this opportunity disappeared?

If the answer does not fit, Orin says no, even if it pays.

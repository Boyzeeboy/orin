# Engagement letter — The Diagnostic

*Draft template. Not legal advice — have a solicitor review before first use.*
*Decisions you need to make are marked **[DECIDE]**.*

**Why this is short.** `Offer.md` prices the Diagnostic under the ~£5k
discretionary threshold precisely so no procurement process is required. A
twelve-page master agreement would hand the client a reason to start one. This
is designed to be read in five minutes and agreed by email reply. The fuller
agreement (`master-services-agreement.md`) comes out when the Build does.

Thank you for asking me to look at [product / system name]. This letter sets out

## 1. What I'll do

A bounded diagnostic of your design system substrate over **[1–2] weeks**,
beginning **[date]**. I'll work through your product, codebase, Figma files and
whatever design or brand guidelines exist, and talk to the developers currently
carrying the design load.

I'll run my token pipeline against your Figma file to audit how much of your
design system actually reaches your code. This runs on my own machine; your
variables are read locally and are not uploaded to any third-party service.

## 2. What you'll get

A written diagnosis covering where your substrate is broken, what it's costing
you in velocity and defects, and what fixing it would involve — scoped and
priced. Before I write it up, I'll walk you through the findings live, because a
generated audit produces evidence rather than conclusions.

The diagnosis is yours to keep and act on, whether or not you engage me further.
There is no obligation to proceed to any subsequent work.

## 3. What I need from you

The diagnostic depends on access, and delays here move the delivery date:

- Read access to the relevant Figma file(s) and repositories
- Up to **[3]** hours of conversation with the developers and whoever owns the
  design decisions
- A named contact who can answer questions within [2] working days

## 4. Fee

**£3,000**, fixed. Not an estimate and not chargeable by the hour — if it takes
me longer than planned, that's my risk, not your invoice.

**[DECIDE]** Payment terms. My recommendation for a two-week fixed engagement:
invoiced in full on commencement, payable within 14 days. The alternative is
50% on commencement and 50% on delivery of the written diagnosis, which is
gentler for a first engagement but means chasing two invoices for £3,000.

Fees exclude VAT [if/when registered]. Any travel or expenses over £[100] will
be agreed with you in advance.

Late payment: interest and costs may be charged under the Late Payment of
Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.

## 5. Confidentiality

I'll keep your code, designs, commercial information and anything else
non-public confidential, and use it only to perform this work. This continues
after the engagement ends. It doesn't apply to information that is already
public, that you tell me is unrestricted, or that I'm required to disclose by
law.

If you'd prefer your own NDA, send it and I'll sign it — that's quicker than
negotiating this clause.

## 6. Ownership

The written diagnosis is yours on payment.

The tooling I use to produce it — my token pipeline, its build machinery and
templates — remains mine. It is used across engagements and isn't part of what
you're buying here. If you go on to commission a Build, the ownership position
for what lands in your repositories is set out in full in my services agreement,
and in short it is this: **you own your instance, I retain the underlying
machinery, and you get a permanent licence to use it.**

## 7. Liability

**[DECIDE]** Liability cap. Recommendation: my total liability is limited to the
fees paid under this engagement (£3,000). Clients sometimes push for a multiple
of fees, or a fixed sum such as £250,000 — do not agree to a cap above what your
professional indemnity cover will meet.

I don't exclude liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for
fraud, or for anything else that can't lawfully be excluded. Subject to that, I'm
not liable for indirect or consequential loss, loss of profit, revenue, or
anticipated savings.

**[DECIDE]** Insurance. Once professional indemnity cover is in place, add a line
here confirming it and the level. **Do not include an insurance warranty until
the policy exists** — warranting cover you don't hold is worse than having no
clause at all.

## 8. Talking about the work

I'd like to be able to describe this work publicly — what the problem was, what
I found, and what changed — naming you as the client. I'll agree the wording with
you before publishing anything, and I won't publish your code, your designs or
anything confidential.

If you'd rather I didn't name you, say so and I'll describe the work without
identifying you.

**[DECIDE]** Keep this clause. Two of your three case studies are constrained by
not having agreed this up front — the Vivo work has to be described as
"contracted via Rethink", and IDEM had to be rebuilt personally before it could
be shown at all. Agreeing it now costs nothing; agreeing it afterwards is a
favour you have to ask.

## 9. If it isn't working

Either of us may end this engagement on **[7]** days' written notice. If you end
it, you pay for work done up to that point. If I end it, I'll refund fees for
work not yet done.

earlier discussion. It's governed by the law of England and Wales, and the
courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.

I'm engaged as an independent contractor, not an employee, and I'm responsible
for my own tax and National Insurance.
